## Draft decisions

The local draft under `gigs/` keeps the observed price ladder while narrowing
the promise to defined workflows, documented interfaces, source code, and
handoff documentation. It removes or avoids guarantees, unverifiable outcome
claims, off-platform contact, and unsupported credentials. It asks for the
four intake facts Seller OS can analyze reliably: outcome, current process,
inputs/interfaces, and expected output.
